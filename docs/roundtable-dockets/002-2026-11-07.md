## Open questions

- Q23 Through which of Clerk's means does the app show Clerk's sign-in form?
  - Reason: ADR 008 mounts `<SignIn />` in a popover of the app's own with `routing="virtual"` so that no step of signing in writes to the address. In `@clerk/react` 6.18.8, which loads `@clerk/ui` 1.42.6, and in `@clerk/clerk-js` 5.128.2, the last of Clerk's major version 5, `Portal` in Clerk's portal module gives `HashRouter` to a mounted component under every value of `routing` but `path`, `virtual` included, and `HashRouter` moves between steps by setting `window.location.hash`, so each step after the first changes the address and adds an entry to the browser's history. Only Clerk's own modals, `openSignIn` among them, run under `VirtualRouter` and leave the address unchanged. Clerk's modal covers the viewport with its backdrop, makes the page behind it inert, locks its scroll, closes on a press on the backdrop, and tells the app nothing when it closes. ADR 008 rejected `routing="hash"`, `openSignIn` as it stands, `openSignIn` restyled through `appearance`, and a sign-in form of the app's own on Clerk's `useSignIn` and `useSignUp`. These facts about Clerk come from reading its packages and are not confirmed by a run in a browser.

## Product decisions

- PD65 The sign-in form shows centred over the screen the learner is on, with that screen dimmed behind it and neither usable nor scrollable until the form closes. A press anywhere on the dimmed screen closes the form. The address stays the same through every step of signing in or of making an account.
  - Facts: under PDR 013, signing in happens in a form over the screen the learner is on, and closing it leaves them there. Under PDR 015, Back on a deck's board returns to the list, and the list's address or a deck's address opened while signed out opens the form. Clerk's form leaves the address unchanged only when it shows centred over a dimmed screen, which it keeps from being used or scrolled and which closes the form when pressed. These facts come from reading Clerk's packages and are not confirmed by a run in a browser.
  - Rejected: the form hung under the "Sign in" button over the undimmed board, with each step of signing in in the address, because each step adds an entry to the browser's history, Back walks through the steps, and after signing in on a deck's address Back stays on the deck once before it reaches the list, against PDR 015; the learner seeing which button opened the form, with the board undimmed, does not outweigh a Back that misleads them. Clerk's centred form moved under "Sign in", because the screen still cannot scroll, the form still closes on a press outside it, and a Clerk update puts the form back in the centre with nothing warning anyone. A form of the app's own under "Sign in", because any step of signing in or of making an account that the app gets wrong or leaves out stops the learner from signing in, and Clerk's form already holds every step.
  - Becomes true: the learner signs in or makes an account without the address changing, and Back and Forward after signing in behave as PDR 015 says. A learner sent by an address meets the form under its heading "Sign in to Sort It", tied to no button, over the dimmed board, which comes back undimmed once they close the form. A stray press on the dimmed screen closes the form and loses what the learner typed in it.
