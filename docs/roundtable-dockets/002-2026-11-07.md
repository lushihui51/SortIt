## Lesser decisions

- LPD4 The learner calls a collection of cards with its dimensions a "deck", and each thing they sort a "card". The words "set" and "item" appear nowhere the learner reads.
  - Facts: under PDR 012, the learner keeps and names several collections. A collection's items already appear as cards. "Board" names the sorting area in every record.
  - Rejected: "board" for the collection, because it already names the sorting area and suggests one sorting session rather than a collection kept. "Set" and "item", by the product owner's choice of words.
  - Becomes true: the learner reads "deck" and "card" on every screen, the error PDR 009 shows included. The vocabulary changes to the new words, and the records keep theirs.
- LPD7 The name refusals read: for a deck name the learner already uses, "You already have a deck called "<Deck>"."; for a deck with an empty name, "A deck needs a name."; for a card name the deck already has, new or renamed, "The deck "<Deck>" already has a card called "<Card>"."; for renaming a card to an empty name, "A card needs a name." Each shows as a line under the field, which keeps what the learner typed.
  - Facts: PDR 009 and PDR 012 refuse these names. Under PDR 009, two decks may each hold a card of one name, so a refusal names what holds the name. The product owner wants every message to name the kind of thing and its name.
  - Rejected: "That name is already taken.", because it does not say what holds the name. Adding "Choose another name.", because the field keeping the typed text already says so.
  - Becomes true: the learner reads in each refusal what holds the name, by kind and by name.
- LPD18 The line PDR 014 shows for a change not kept holds one line per change not kept, in two sentences: what was not kept, "Your new <kind> "<Name>" was not kept.", "Renaming the <kind> "<Name>" to "<New name>" was not kept." or "Your change to the <kind> "<Name>" was not kept."; then why, the refusal LPD7 and VC25 to VC28 give for a name taken, or "It was deleted on another device." or "The <kind> "<Name>" was deleted on another device." for something deleted. A Save answers kept in part under PDR 014 gives one line for each card or category it leaves out: "Your answer for the card "<Card>" was not kept." or "Your answers in the category "<Category>" were not kept.", then "It was deleted on another device." A later change to a thing whose making was not kept gives no line of its own, and the line for its making opens "Your new <kind> "<Name>" and your later changes to it were not kept." Each line stays until the learner leaves the screen it shows on, by opening a deck, going back to the list of decks, or reloading.
  - Facts: under PDR 014, a line names what was not kept and why, so the learner can redo it. Under LPD7, every refusal names the kind of thing and its name. The product owner wants every message to name the kind of thing and its name. The line for a deck that cannot be opened stays until the learner leaves the list. Under PDR 014, a Save answers kept late keeps the answers of the cards and categories the deck still holds. A thing whose making was not kept is not on the screen, so a later change to it finds nothing to change.
  - Rejected: one line for all, "Some changes you made while offline were not kept.", because the learner cannot tell which changes to redo. Each line without its reason, because the learner cannot tell whether to retry under another name. A × on each line, because it adds a button the line for a deck that cannot be opened does not have. Fading after a few seconds, because a learner busy dragging, or reading slowly, misses which change to redo. One line per card for a deleted category, because a category of many cards fills the top of the board with lines saying one thing. "Your change to the card "<Card>" was not kept." for an answer left out, because the learner pressed Save answers and does not read which act was lost. A line for each later change to a thing whose making was not kept, with "Its making was not kept." as why, because one lost thing makes several lines naming one cause.
  - Becomes true: the learner reads which change to redo, and why, in the words of the refusals they already know, at their own pace, and both kinds of line go away the same way. Several lines take room from the board until the learner leaves it. The learner reads one line for each thing lost. A learner who renamed a thing after making it reads it under the name they first gave it.
  - Drawings: 9
